Safe mode

Its own endpoint, its own confirmation — never part of Save

Safe mode is the difference between “this app can change my Plex watch history” and “it cannot”. It is stored separately from every other setting on this page and is not written by the Save button, so it can never be flipped by accident while changing something else.
